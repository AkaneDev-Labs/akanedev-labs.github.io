# Support AkaneDevLabs

AkaneDevLabs is an independent Australian development company focused on making computing more open, accessible and available to everyone.

We work on software, hardware concepts, open-source projects and experimental technologies. However, developing these ideas takes time, equipment and resources.

If you believe in what we're trying to achieve, you can support our work through a voluntary donation.

[Donate Here](https://buymeacoffee.com/akanedevlabs)

## What will your donation help fund?

Donations help support the continued development and operation of AkaneDevLabs.

Depending on our current needs, contributions may go towards:

### Hardware Development
- Electronic components and development boards.
- Prototyping and testing equipment.
- PCB manufacturing.
- Hardware research and experimentation.

### Software Development
- Development tools and software licences.
- Testing environments.
- Software infrastructure.
- Open-source project development.

### Infrastructure and Operations
- Hosting and server expenses.
- Domain registrations.
- Development infrastructure.
- Equipment maintenance and upgrades.

### Research and Experimentation
- Exploring new computing architectures.
- Instruction set architecture (ISA) translation research.
- Hardware compatibility testing.
- Experimental computing projects.

### General Support
You can also contribute without specifying a particular project.

General donations will be allocated wherever they are most useful to the continued operation and development of AkaneDevLabs.

## Donation Options

You can choose how you would like to support AkaneDevLabs:

- **General Donation** – Support the company as a whole.
- **Hardware Development** – Help fund hardware research and prototyping.
- **Software Development** – Support software and open-source development.
- **Infrastructure** – Help cover hosting, equipment and operational expenses.
- **Research and Development** – Support experimental technologies and future projects.

These are suggested allocation preferences. We may need to redirect contributions where necessary to cover project requirements or operational costs.

## Important Information

**Donations are voluntary contributions.**

Making a donation does not:

- Guarantee access to unreleased software or hardware.
- Guarantee early access to projects.
- Guarantee any physical or digital rewards.
- Provide ownership or equity in AkaneDevLabs.
- Provide influence over development decisions.
- Guarantee that a particular project will be completed.
- Establish any contractual relationship beyond the donation itself.

Donations are intended to support development and operational expenses. They are not investments, purchases or financial contributions with an expected return.

We cannot guarantee that any particular project will be completed, released or successful.
