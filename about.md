## Technology for all.

AkaneDev Labs is an independent Australian software development company built around exploring ideas, creating things, and making technology accessible.

We work across several areas of development, from software and hardware to games, modding, and publishing. Some projects become full products, others start as experiments, and some exist simply because we wanted to see if an idea could work.

## What we do.

### Software

We develop software ranging from everyday tools and utilities to more ambitious applications. We enjoy solving problems, exploring new approaches, and building things that are useful.

### Hardware

Not everything happens in software. We experiment with hardware, embedded systems, electronics, and projects that bring physical and digital technology together.

[Checkout AkaneDevLabs Computing](/computing/)

### Modding

We enjoy extending and modifying existing software and games. This includes creating modifications, experimenting with game systems, and contributing to the modding community.

### Games

We develop games and interactive experiences, exploring different mechanics, technologies, and ways to play.

### Publishing

We help bring games and modifications to their audiences. This includes publishing our own projects and providing publishing services for other developers.

### Experiments

Not every idea needs to become a product. We maintain room for prototypes, technical experiments, unusual concepts, and projects that exist purely to explore what's possible.

## How we work.

We believe good products start with curiosity and grow through experimentation and community involvement.

We aren't limited to one platform, technology, or discipline. We use the tools that make sense for the project, learn as we go, and aren't afraid to try something unconventional.

Some ideas make perfect sense on paper. Others don't. We build them anyway.
