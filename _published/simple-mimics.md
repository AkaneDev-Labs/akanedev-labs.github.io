## About Simple Mimics

This mod adds Mimics to your game. They can copy Player Nametags, Skins, and even voice clips through [Simple Voice Chat](https://modrinth.com/plugin/simple-voice-chat) (Required).

It requires no additional setup and is ideal for use with friends or even playing alone.

## Compatibility

### Confirmed compatible with

- [Essential](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/essential-mod) — Accepts skin changing via the Essential mod.
- [Create Aeronautics](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-aeronautics) — Does not cause any known issues.

### Could be compatible with

- Any mod that changes your skins. We haven't confirmed this, though.
- Most mods :D

## Known Bugs

1. Simple Mimics version `1.2.0` has a bug that prevents Mimics from spawning.
2. Simple Mimics version `1.4.0` has a bug where players get kicked from a server when sending a message due to a programming mistake.

### Fix

Update to the latest version to resolve both bugs.
