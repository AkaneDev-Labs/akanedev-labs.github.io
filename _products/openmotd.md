## About OpenMOTD

OpenMOTD was created after AkaneDev learnt that Minecraft servers could be banned by Mojang/Microsoft based on their MOTD.

It was created to give server owners the freedom to display whatever MOTD they want, as it should be.

We decided that this shouldn't just be a Minecraft-specific solution. It should be a real, open standard that anyone can implement and use anywhere.
